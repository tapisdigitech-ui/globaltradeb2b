    Is it free to list my business on GlobalTradeB2B?

    Yes. A basic business listing is free. You can add products and catalogues and receive buyer enquiries at no cost.

    How do I post a buying requirement?

    Select Post Your Requirement, enter the product, quantity, specifications and delivery location, and submit. Relevant suppliers send you quotations.

    How can I claim my company if it is already listed?

    Open your company profile and choose Claim Your Company. We verify your business details before giving you access to manage the page.

    How can I contact suppliers on the platform?

    Use Contact Supplier on any profile or product, or post a requirement and matching suppliers will send quotations.
